Underline tabs are the pick. This round fixes the pinned Files header, swaps in the real hugeicons glyphs (Archive01 for chapters, PanelLeft for the toggle, and the rest of the chrome), and draws the page at each Tailwind breakpoint, including the one-column version. E is folded away at the bottom for later.

What is fixed now

  • Viewer full height on the left. Its header starts with the layout toggle, then the workspace menu, then the open file.
  • Workspace menu. A ghost-hover trigger on the name, no resting background. The dropdown lists the other workspaces with the current one checked, and ends with a left-aligned ghost-hover Workspace settings button, size sm. No back link, no All workspaces. The purple card is gone.
  • Layout toggle is the PanelLeft glyph with no active styling; the extra column makes the state obvious. Shown from xl up only.
  • Tabs are the underline Tabs component with Files added. When Files is pinned to the left, its panel gets a card-title header instead of a lone tab.
  • Action icons, B style, always at their full width. From lg up the tab row ends with the active tab's own actions (collapse chapters on Files, history and new chat on Chat), then a circled plus and a settings gear. The plus opens Upload or import, New file, New chapter; New file opens the Add source dialog on its Create tab, a placeholder today. The icons never shrink: when the row is narrow the tabs scroll instead, scrollbar hidden, with a fade at the far edge like shadcn's scroll-fade. Below lg the actions fold into a dots menu next to the plus.
  • Chapter rows. A chevron at the far left shows open or collapsed, then Archive01, then the name; a collapsed chapter shows its item count.
  • Composer pill reads Curate mode.
  • Unchanged from round one: top inset bar, one panel at a time, three columns pins Files left, layout remembered per browser, panel opens on Files when nothing is open.

Breakpoints

Tailwind defaults: sm 640, md 768, lg 1024, xl 1280, 2xl 1536. Three layouts cover them. The top inset bar already goes full width below lg on the other pages, with the nav hamburger inside it, so the one-column page reuses that instead of inventing a mobile header.
