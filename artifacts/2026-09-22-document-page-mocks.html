BIOLOGY · STUDY NOTES

Cell biology

How cells move substances across their membranes.

The membrane is selective

A phospholipid bilayer separates the cell from its surroundings. Small, nonpolar molecules pass through more easily than ions and large polar molecules.

Remember the direction

Passive transport follows the concentration gradient. Active transport moves against it and requires energy.

Three ways to cross

ProcessWhat movesEnergy
DiffusionSmall moleculesNot required
OsmosisWaterNot required
Active transportIons and moleculesRequired

DEPARTMENT OF BIOLOGY 1

Try Edit, then draw on the page. Click Draw or Shape to open its dropdown. Text opens an input; enter words and click the page to place them. Color opens swatches. Select returns to text selection. Shape supports rectangles and ellipses. Eraser replaces the pointer with a large circle; drag it across a mark to remove it. Undo and Redo use local session history. The page label is plain text. On narrow screens, scroll the middle tools horizontally; the toolbar stays on one row.

Proposed behavior

Keep the header smallUse the existing navigationBack icon, then the document icon and title. Remove the Create/Files label, workspace selector, panel toggle and slash. Keep View/Edit and the document action menu.

One fixed PDF toolbarA plain Page x of x label stays on the left; zoom-out and zoom-in icons stay on the right. Drawing tools and Undo/Redo occupy the center. Below lg, the zoom icons are hidden. Edit enables selection, Draw, Text, Shape, Color, Eraser and Undo/Redo. One toolbar row stays above the scrolling pages, without page arrows or an extra tool-hint row. No toolbar appears on document clicks.

Draw, text and shapesDraw offers Pen and Highlighter. Text places a text annotation. Shape offers Rectangle and Ellipse. Color applies to the active drawing, text or shape tool. Escape returns to selection.

Large eraser cursorA visible 48 px radius ring replaces the pointer over the PDF. Hold and drag to erase annotations touched by the circle. The original PDF content is unaffected. In this mock, intersecting annotation objects are removed whole.

Undo/Redo stays localHistory belongs to the open PDF session and resets when it closes or reloads. Switching View/Edit keeps that history while the page stays open. Existing private annotation persistence is separate from undo history; it is not being made temporary.

Shared in workspace and library pagesThe same PDF toolbar goes inside the shared document frame in both contexts. The toolbar stays on one row: plain Page x of x on the left, drawing and history actions centered, and zoom icons on the right. Below lg, zoom is hidden. The center tools scroll horizontally with fading edges and no scrollbar. New library opens still default to View.
