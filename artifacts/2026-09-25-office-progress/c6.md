## PPTX (no publication)

The fork's PPTX editor takes text only from single-character `keydown` events
(`packages/pptx-react/src/PptxEditor.tsx:1617-1631`); it has no paste, `beforeinput` or IME path,
Playwright cannot press non-US keys, and speaker notes produce no effects (measured: a 14k-character
notes edit gives 0 effects). Typing into one text box runs at 27-50 ms per character and slows as
the box grows, so reaching 3,000 net tokens would take more than 20 minutes. The developer chose to
keep the PPTX journey without a publication: store-only upload, the README's edits, the saved
export (every part except slides 3 and 18 byte-identical), the owner's editor (speaker note),
the editor account's viewer (slides 1, 3 and 18), the charge (source alone after opening, the rule
after the edits) and zero provider calls. PPTX publication stays covered by the basic PPTX journey
and the fork's rebase tests. No PPTX helper became dead: `editRich`, `expectRichContent` (edit and
view) and `assertRichPreserved` all still run for PPTX.

## Where the plan's list and the fixture README differ

The journeys follow the README, which asks for more than the plan: the marker paragraph, chart
parts with cached values and embedded workbooks (byte-identical), picture hashes, page breaks (5),
the footer reference; XLSX sheet names, frozen panes at `C3`, `D4` = 5.4 with `C4` = 10 and the
Summary drawing-to-chart chain.

One deviation: **the six DOCX `w:eastAsia="ja-JP"` tags are not asserted.** At the checked-out
engine (`vendor/betteroffice` at `e912e8e0`) an export drops all six even without edits (6 → 0),
which is the filed DOCX-properties task the plan lists under "Not in this round". Asserting it
fails the DOCX journey, and `maxFailures: 1` then stops the suite. If you want it anyway, it is one
line in `assertRichPreserved`: `assert.equal(xml.match(/w:eastAsia="ja-JP"/g)?.length, 6)`.
