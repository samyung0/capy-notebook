Round 3. Picked so far: 1A for questions, 1C for questions that need a split view, 2B for editing, 4A for the question bank page, 5A and 6A. Changes this round:

  • The question bank page is one page for learners and reviewers. Editors flip it into edit mode with the same toggle as the workspace header.

  • Embeds in notes have no type label and no border until selected.
  • The floating toolbar in part 2 and in notes is one shared component.
  • The chart editor's data header is grey, and a focused formula now hugs its text.

The return of the city bee

ATwenty years ago, a beehive on a city rooftop would have puzzled most building managers. Today several European capitals keep registers of urban hives, and some count more than a thousand.

BSupporters argue that parks, gardens and balcony plants give bees a wider mix of flowers than farmland planted with a single crop. In one study, honey from city hives contained pollen from almost twice as many plant species as honey from nearby farms.

CNot everyone is convinced. Ecologists warn that honeybees compete with wild bees for the same flowers, and that a sudden rise in hives can leave too little food for both. Several cities now limit how many hives may be kept per square kilometre.

DBeekeepers themselves are divided on what should happen next. Some want registration to become compulsory everywhere, while others fear that paperwork will push new keepers away before they learn the basics.

2Editing a question

The question dialog: every block and part on the left, the question as students see it on the right. Clicking a row, or Edit on the toolbar, opens that block's editor from part 3 in the same dialog.

B

Outline and preview

  • Rows drag to reorder the way the Files panel does it: the whole row moves, and an accent line shows where it will land. No handles.
  • Each row has a ⋮ menu. A part's menu switches its answer type, opens its answer and marking scheme, moves it or removes it.
  • The + at the top adds a block or a part below the selected row.
  • The selected block gets a toolbar above it in the preview: edit, replace, move, duplicate, delete.
  • A part's marks come from its marking scheme, so the row shows them read-only.

3Block editors

Clicking a block swaps the dialog body to its editor. The back link returns to the question without closing the dialog. Notes open the same editors in their own dialog. Remove and Save sit together on the right, like the app's other dialogs.

Text

  • Words and formulas edit in the same box. Clicking a formula edits it in place with MathLive.
  • MathLive turns typing into notation: 5/6 becomes a fraction, sqrt a root, ^ a power, >= becomes ≥.
  • The toolbar only holds what typing can't easily reach: sums, integrals, limits, nth roots, logs, vectors.
  • Saved as text with $…$ around each formula, which is what the viewer reads.

4Question bank page

One page for everyone. Learners, and anyone without the editor role, only get view mode. Editors get the toggle, the same one as in the workspace header, which switches to edit mode for reviewing and editing.

A

View mode

  • The question shows with answer boxes and nothing else: no answers, marking schemes or worked solutions.
  • Check answer adds the question to a learning plan if it isn't on one, grades it and saves the attempt there. The details are a follow-up.
  • The list shows the learning plan's result: a check for correct, a cross for incorrect, an empty circle for not attempted.
  • No app sidebar. The top inset bar sits in the panel's notch, as on other pages without the sidebar.

The quadratic formula

For ax2+bx+c=0ax^2 + bx + c = 0 with a≠0a \ne 0, the roots are x=−b±b2−4ac2ax = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} and they are real when the discriminant is at least zero.

Δ=b2−4ac\Delta = b^2 - 4ac

If Δ<0\Delta < 0, the graph never meets the xx-axis.
