Question 37 marks

The figure shows the graph of y = x² − 4x + 3.

(a)

Write down the coordinates of the vertex of the graph.

[2]
Answer

(2, −1)

Marking scheme
  • Correct x-coordinate, 21
  • Correct y-coordinate, −11
Worked solution

Complete the square: y = (x − 2)² − 1. The minimum is at (2, −1).

(b)

The line y = k touches the graph at exactly one point. Find the value of k.

[2]
Answer

k = −1

Marking scheme
  • Uses the y-coordinate of the vertex1
  • States k = −11
Worked solution

A horizontal tangent passes through the vertex, so k = −1.

(c)

Solve x² − 4x + 3 < 0.

[3]
Answer

1 < x < 3

Marking scheme
  • Factorises into (x − 1)(x − 3)1
  • Identifies the roots 1 and 31
  • Chooses the interval between the roots1
Worked solution

The upward-opening parabola lies below the x-axis between its roots, so 1 < x < 3.
