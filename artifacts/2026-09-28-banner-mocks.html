Banner mocks

Classroom latte tokens. Part 1 is your spec for the file banners, one version for sign-off. Part 2 is the picked dashboard slot style (A). Part 3 is the workspace side: an entry toast plus a header status icon, one version.

1. File banners

  • Flat strip under the file header, full width, no rounded card.
  • No leading icon. Grey for info and progress, red tint background for errors, fg text on both.
  • Close × on the right hides it until the next page or refresh. The progress bar is gone.
  • Buttons small ghost, underlined, on a new line, aligned right, no icons. Text is the normal fg colour on every tone.

2. Dashboard slot (A picked)

The account and connection banners leave the app shell (no more layout shift) and take the default banner's place on the dashboard. One card at a time, in this order: frozen, grace, offline, reconnecting, then the default. Suspended or deleted accounts keep today's full-screen block.

ATone tint. Red for frozen, amber for grace and connection. Faint icon top right like the sparkles. Small ghost buttons on a new line, left aligned.

3. Workspace

The in-workspace storage banner goes away. Entering a workspace fires one toast per problem. The header status slot next to the title shows the wifi-off icon while offline. When the owner is out of storage or your account is frozen, a red triangle ghost button replaces it, and clicking it opens the dialog. The status slot is added to file headers too, since only notes have it today.
