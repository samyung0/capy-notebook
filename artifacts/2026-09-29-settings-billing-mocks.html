Settings and Billing & usage, round 5

Round 5 only touches the Subscription tab: the grey line under the plan name is gone and invoices sit below. Everything else is unchanged from round 4. Rendered with the app's real Tailwind output and the sidebar and top bar captured from the running app (classroom, latte, 1440 wide).

  • Every tab starts with a header: t-card-title plus one line of description.
  • Rows copy the workspace sharing dialog: title and hint on the left, dropdown or switch on the right, dropdowns with a leading icon.
  • Appearance is now Customizations (Theme, Mode). Language moved into Account, below the profile Save; it still applies on change.
  • LLM rows have hints. Save (dark) and Remove (danger) are md buttons with rounded-input, the same height as the sharing dialog's Copy button next to its input.
  • Usage follows your reference: title and "x of y used" on one line, the bar, then the hint. Credits is one stacked bar coloured by area, with a legend underneath.
  • Area colours reuse the five usercolor tints (purple, blue, green, amber, coral). All five already exist in latte, macchiato and mocha, which are catppuccin palettes, so no new tokens.
  • Subscription lists Stripe invoices below the plan (Date, Due, Total, Status, View). It uses the same table as Detailed usage. Invoice amounts in the mock are sample data.
  • Subscription has no pricing cards. Free users get Upgrade to Pro (straight to Stripe checkout), Pro users get Manage subscription (Stripe portal). Plan comparison lives on the landing site.
  • Dashboard heading: the streak line becomes "What would you like to learn today?".
