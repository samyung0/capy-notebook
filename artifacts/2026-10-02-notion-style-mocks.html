The cell

SourceLecture 01.pdfTopicCell biology

Cells are the smallest units of life. Every cell maintains an internal environment, uses energy and carries genetic information.

What every cell has in common

Although cells vary in size and structure, they share four essential components.

  • Plasma membrane controls what enters and leaves.
  • Cytoplasm provides the setting for chemical reactions.
  • DNA stores the instructions for growth and reproduction.
  • Ribosomes build proteins from those instructions.
Prokaryotes have no membrane-bound nucleus. Eukaryotes keep their DNA inside one.

Structure follows function

A cell's shape and organelles reflect the work it does. Muscle cells contain many mitochondria because contraction requires energy.

Why do muscle cells have so many mitochondria?
Capy

More mitochondria help muscle cells sustain repeated contractions.

Hover and motion

Most controls fade their background in 100ms. Some list rows use 20ms.

No translation, scaling or animated shadow on hover. Reduced motion is respected.

Inter

Selected for Capy. UI labels 14px, reading text 16px and document titles 40px.

Measurements and scope

Light colors: page #ffffff, sidebar #f9f8f7, primary text #2c2c2b, secondary text #7d7a75, subtle border #e6e5e3. Topbar 44px. Page title 40/48px at weight 700; document text 16/24px. UI labels 14px. Default hover uses a faint warm neutral overlay.

Notion uses its own icon artwork. These mocks keep Capy's existing Hugeicons and workspace icons, matching optical size and placement. Inter is the selected font for this round. Popup entry animation here is a proposal; only the hover and side-peek timings above were measured.

All people, documents, messages and billing values in this mock are fictional. Controls demonstrate appearance only.
