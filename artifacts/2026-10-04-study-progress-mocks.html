Theme

1. Study tab (B)

Sections show only when they have something in them. Done counts per chapter, from C. Quick review sits above Done: one flashcard at a time from the sets in progress, among cards missed at least once, the one most likely forgotten first; click the card to flip it, then rate it like on the review page. Ratings are recorded the same way, so a card rated Good drops down the list. Track progress and the counts stay at the bottom; Reset moves to Workspace settings, Danger; viewers, who cannot open settings today, get the dialog with only Reset.

2. Marks in the file panel (B)

Marks sit far enough left that the row's ⋮ shows beside them on hover. Cell biology mindmap is untracked, shown as muted text. The row menu gains Mark as read / Mark as unread and Stop tracking / Start tracking.

3. Mark as read in the header

An icon right of the view/edit toggle on every file, note, quiz and flashcard set: Mark as read (open book with a check), and once read a closed book with a check in the marks' green; clicking it again marks unread. Quiz attempts and flashcard ratings mark items done or started on their own, as in the plan. The header menu has the same items. Question, card and known counts leave the header.

4. Review in Learning, materials in Files

Learning gets a Review tab beside Past results: one row and one Review per workspace in progress, no per-set review. The Study tab's Review starts the same session for its workspace, and Back returns to where it started. The Files page gets tabs Files, Blocks and Trash: Blocks holds notes, quizzes and flashcard sets (the Create page's list) with New block, and Create leaves the navigation. The end of a session and question sizes come back later; the end screen stays as is.
