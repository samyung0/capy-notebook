Exams and topics

Cover art

Thirteen styles, all in T1. C1 to C6 are ours, so no licence questions. C7 to C13 come from open-source projects.

C1Subject symbols
Glyphs that say what the exam is about: maths symbols, Latin letters, kana. Picked by exam kind, scattered from the exam id. Ours, generated · Ours
C2Icon doodles
A chat-wallpaper style doodle of study icons chosen per exam. Hugeicons (already shipped) · MIT
C3Bookshelf
Book spines in shades of the exam colour, name on the empty left side. Ours, generated · Ours
C4Exercise paper
Graph paper for maths exams, ruled paper for English, genkō squares for Japanese, with one handwritten line. Ours, generated · Ours
C5Chalkboard
The same paper idea on a dark board in chalk. Same for every exam except the line, so colour comes from the tile only. Ours, generated · Ours
C6Big type
The exam code set huge and cropped behind the name. No art at all. Ours · Ours
C7GeoPattern
16 generators; the exam id picks the shapes and nudges the colour. Pinned here per exam. geopattern by Jason Long (GitHub Guides backgrounds) · MIT
C8Hero Patterns
87 hand-made repeating patterns. Graph paper, wiggle, jigsaw and seigaiha clouds picked per exam. Hero Patterns by Steve Schoger · CC BY 4.0, needs a Credits line
C9DiceBear Glass
See-through overlapping shapes from the exam id. DiceBear · CC0
C10DiceBear Shapes
Flat geometric shapes from the exam id, in three shades of the exam colour. DiceBear · CC0
C11DiceBear Rings
Segmented rings, cropped large so only arcs show. DiceBear · CC0
C12Mesh gradient
Soft blurred blobs in shades of the exam colour. Static, no animation. Boring Avatars (marble) · MIT
C13Waves
Round 2 baseline. Fixed images, so exams share from a set of 11. Our waves set (DiceBear remix, already in the app) · CC0
Not mockedPhotos
Real photos (a stack of textbooks, a calculator, a lecture hall) from Unsplash: free for commercial use with no attribution, but they cannot be resold as-is, and photos with brand logos or people need care. Editors would upload them through the image option. Photos are busier than any style above, so they would need T1's shade or T2.

Setting a cover

One dialog for bank editors, opened from the exam's ⋮ menu in edit mode: style, its option, colour, Shuffle for a new seed, or Upload image. On Save we render the cover once and store it as an image in the public question bank bucket, so learners load one small file and the app ships no pattern libraries.
