Workspace cover picker

2026-10-08. The Cover row in Workspace settings opens a picker: styles on the left, the preview and the style's own options on the right. Today one "Maths / Languages / Japanese" dropdown drives three different things and Geometric and Patterns hide their patterns behind Shuffle, so each style now names its own variants:

StyleVariants
Subject symbolsMaths, Languages, Japanese (which glyphs)
Icon doodlesScience, Languages, Japanese (which icons)
Exercise paperGraph paper, Ruled, Genkō squares
Geometric16 patterns (hexagons, chevrons, plaid…)
Patterns18 patterns (graph paper, jigsaw, clouds…)
Bookshelf, Big typeNone; colour and Shuffle only

The stored config does not change (kind or pattern); only the labels and the way you pick them. Every option shows as a small strip of the real art in the chosen colour.
