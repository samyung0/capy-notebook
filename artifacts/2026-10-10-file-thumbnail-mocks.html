Notes

  • Render cost (this PC). PPTX slide 1 took 50–700 ms to open and render. XLSX A1:H30 took 0.5–0.9 s, and 8.6 s for large-gradebook.xlsx. PDF page 1 took 5–250 ms.
  • DOCX on the server needs text measured elsewhere first. The upload-time DOCX thumbnail comes from the parser's LibreOffice PDF, and edits after that are captured by the editor's worker.
  • Diagram previews need a small layout of our own for each Mermaid type: flowchart, sequence, state, pie, timeline and mindmap. Types without one fall back to the icon, so Mermaid itself never loads on a list page.
  • Preview field: about 600 bytes per material. Notes keep the first blocks and the first image's small copy, quizzes the first question and the question count, flashcards the first front and the card count.
