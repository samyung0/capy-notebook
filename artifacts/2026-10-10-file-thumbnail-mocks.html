Things the mock shows

  • Width. The cards in A and B are 220–300 CSS px wide. A 200px image is stretched in them even on a 1× screen, and on a 2× screen it covers about a third of the pixels. Turn on 400px to compare. C's 84px box is the only shape where 200px is enough on 2× screens.
  • Phone. A and B switch to two columns on phones, as Drive does. Today's grid is one column at 375px, so each thumbnail would be about 340px wide.
  • Dark mode. White pages glare on the dark canvas. Dim pages is a CSS filter at display time, so the stored image does not change.
  • Render cost (this PC). PPTX slide 1 took 50–700 ms to open and render. XLSX A1:H30 took 0.5–0.9 s, but 8.6 s for large-gradebook.xlsx, where opening the workbook is the cost. PDF page 1 took 5–250 ms.
  • Fonts. The Japanese deck rendered as tofu until I registered Noto Sans JP. Server renders need the CJK fonts from fonts-cjk.
  • DOCX on the server. The Python binding's layout() needs text measured somewhere else, the browser or ooxml-text, so a server-side DOCX render is not a single call. The upload-time DOCX thumbnail can come from the parser's LibreOffice PDF instead. Edits after that are captured by the editor's worker.
