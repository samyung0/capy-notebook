Flashcard flip transition

Today a click flips the card instantly; only moving to the next card animates (throw away + rise, kept as is below). Four flip options on the motion-token scale. Click a card to flip it (and again to flip back), rate to move on. All variants show the compact card the note embed and Quick review now use; the study page uses the same motion on its taller card. Reduced motion: every variant becomes an instant swap.

BSquash turn

Edge-on in --motion-duration-quick (150ms, ease-in-out) dipping to --motion-scale-popup, content swaps, turns in with --motion-duration-fast (250ms) on the menu morph's --motion-ease-spring. Snappier; never shows a mirrored half-turn; one face mounted at a time.

CText swap

The card stays put; front leaves up --motion-distance-micro with --motion-blur-small in --motion-duration-quick, back enters from below in --motion-duration-fast, both --motion-ease-in-out (the text-swap tokens). Quietest; best in tight spots (Quick review), least "card-like".

DTip over

As A but around the horizontal axis (rotateX 180°): the card tips toward you like an index card, in line with the stack's vertical rise. Same tokens as A.
