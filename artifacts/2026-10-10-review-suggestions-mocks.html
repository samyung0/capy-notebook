Round 2: changes and open points

  1. Mode names: Struggling becomes Tricky (light, no blame), Settle it in becomes Just learned, Fading stays. Difficult reads like a property of the material rather than of your memory.
  2. Mode colours: no red. Tricky peach, Fading blue (cool, like ink fading), Just learned green. Colour only on the small label in row three, so the list stays calm.
  3. Items everywhere. Cards and questions both count as items in the score, so the count does too.
  4. Review again shows for workspaces with any recorded session there. Is that right, or only when the last session was manual?
  5. Flashcards in Past reviews: same x / y shape as quizzes, where x is cards rated Good or Easy. A quiz cell is the summed question score over questions answered.
  6. Unfinished sessions (left before the end) stay in Past reviews with what was answered, "stopped after 5 of 11" under the score. Hide them instead?
  7. Study tab row: a suggestion replaces "Refresh your knowledge" with "<group> is tricky / fading / just learned" plus the reason; Review starts that suggestion. With none, today's row stays.
