Shell layout: sidebar rail and top bar placement

Agreed so far: the notch layout (top bar in the panel's top-right notch) stays from 1280px (xl). From 1024px to 1279px the page is one column with the full sidebar. From 640px to 1023px the sidebar condenses to an icon rail. Below 640px (phones) the sidebar stays a drawer opened from a menu button. Frames are drawn at 60% scale with the Dashboard as the example page.

Pick A, B or C (or mix: e.g. B on desktop, A on phones). The 1280px+ notch layout is unchanged in every option.

For comparison. From 1024px the top bar sits in the panel's notch; below it the bar is its own grey container above the panel and the sidebar is gone.

No separate container: search, bell and the profile pill sit in the panel's title row, right of the page title. The sidebar and rail hold navigation only. On phones the row is menu, then search, bell and an avatar without the name, with the title below.

Profile at the foot of the sidebar (avatar at the foot of the rail); search and bell in the panel's title row. On phones: menu, search, bell in the panel, profile at the drawer's foot.
