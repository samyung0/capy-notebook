Worked solution in the question edit dialog

Question 6 (matching, 4 marks). The same behaviour in every variant:

  • The worked solution shows in the body under its part by default. No click needed.
  • The left "Worked solution" row stops being a toggle. It becomes a 36px header row (the ⋮ fits now) with "Add block", and its blocks are always listed below it.
  • A wavy line sits above the solution in both columns. It marks content learners don't see until they check their answer.

The variants differ only in what sits on the line.
