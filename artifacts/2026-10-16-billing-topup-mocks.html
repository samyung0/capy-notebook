Billing: top-ups, auto-reload, spending limit

Mocks for the 2026-10-16 billing decisions, drawn with the app's own CSS (latte, dark toggle on the right). Layout C is picked. Version 5: layout C and R2, purchased credits, threshold in credits, reloads checked against the monthly limit, owed credits after a failed reload, and a limit removed by setting it to $0.

Still open

  1. Overrun without a failed reload (for example a long upload finishing past zero): keep absorbing it as today, or carry it as owed credits too, so one rule covers both? The mock only shows the failed-reload case.
  2. Proposal: when Stripe stops retrying, the invoice is voided and the owed credits stay. Auto-reload stays on and charges again the next time credits reach 0 after credits come in.

1. Usage tab (layout C)

Pro account: 6,420 of 10,000 monthly credits used, 4,070 purchased credits (top-ups plus the starter credits). One bar; a line marks where purchased credits start, with its label centred under it. Top up opens the Credits section of the Subscription tab.

2. Subscription tab (R2)

Credits, now a larger heading, holds Buy credits and Monthly limit side by side with a divider, invoices below. Top up charges the typed amount through Stripe Checkout ($8 to $100); the field shows what it buys. The auto-reload line and Edit share one row on every width; the line wraps instead. Defaults are reload $10 when credits reach 0.

3. Defaults, reload notices and owed credits

Auto-reload off and no limit set (the defaults for new accounts). Notices sit above the two panels on Subscription and above the bars on Usage, styled like the app's callouts. Auto-reload never turns itself off. When a reload fails, the credits used while it was charging stay owed (a negative balance) until credits come in; next month they come off the new monthly credits first.

4. Dialogs and the manual top-up rule

Cancel on the left, the accented action on the right, a destructive action next to it. Edit opens the auto-reload dialog (reference layout, threshold in credits); turning the switch on for the first time opens it too. A saved method that is not instant is refused there. The monthly limit has no Remove button: setting it to $0 removes it. Pressing Top up while Stripe is charging an auto-reload (a few seconds) shows the warning; the toast replaces it when both together would pass the monthly limit. After a failed reload, a top-up just goes ahead and cancels Stripe's retry.

5. Free account

200 monthly credits, then the purchased credits that start with the 800 starter credits.
