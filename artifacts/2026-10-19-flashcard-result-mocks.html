The front is now a centred header pinned to the top of the card, muted, with the rule across under it; the answer is centred in the space left. 2 · Layout A

The time now shows once, in the stat line; the header keeps the date and workspace. Relearned is gone. The +2 since 11 Oct chip compares with the set's previous attempt. Cards use the B1 back with the Sort menu (S1).

3 · The order control

S1 (used above): the Sort menu the Workspaces, Create and Files pages already use: a ghost "Sort: As studied" button with no border until hovered, icon only on phones. Its menu has As studied and By rating (Again first); choosing the active one again reverses it, as on the list pages.

S2: no button at all. The heading reads "Cards in study order" and opens the same menu. Quietest, but the heading being clickable is easy to miss.
