Round 6: the Files page's chip filter (Kind only), flashcard results in the app's result colours with the bar chart and no square grid.

1 · Study progress

Rows as on the phone: icon, name, one muted line ("3 attempts · 10 questions · last 16 Oct", "3 attempts · 18 cards"), the latest result, a toggle for past results, the state mark and a ⋮ on hover (shown on Osmosis notes.txt).

Clicking a row opens the file, or for a quiz or set its latest result page; one never attempted opens the quiz or set itself. The toggle lists the last 3 results with date and time, each opening its result, then a link to the rest in All results (Cell biology basics has 5).

Filter is the Files page's own (open in the desktop frame): one Kind section of chips, no Workspace section since the page is one workspace. Show stays a dropdown beside it: All items, Not read, Read, Not tracked.

2 · End of a flashcard attempt

Today the study page ends on "Done for now" and Study again. Proposed: the same page shows the attempt's result. The score is cards rated Good or Easy ("14 / 18 good"), then one bar per rating with its count, no square grid. Colours are the result colours the quiz result page uses (red, amber, blue, green). Worth another look lists the Hard and Again cards with a dot in their rating's colour; a card rated Again then Good says so. Study these 4 starts an attempt of only those.

3 · A flashcard attempt's result page

Each recorded attempt gets a page like a quiz attempt's, under Learning / All results, opened from the progress page and All results: /flashcards/attempts/$attemptId.

4 · All results

Quiz and flashcard attempts in one list: the first column becomes Name with the item's icon, the Filter gains Type (quizzes, flashcards), and a flashcard score reads "14 / 18 good".

What it needs and open questions

  • New record: flashcard attempts. Today a set's study page writes only ratings (review_states, review_log), so nothing knows where one attempt starts or ends. Proposed: flashcard_attempts (id from the browser, user, set, started_at, last_answer_at, cards served in order) and flashcard_attempt_answers (card, first rating, later ratings), written on the first rating like review sessions. An attempt left early keeps the cards rated so far ("6 / 10 good"), with no "stopped" label.
  • Quiz time is new too. attempts keeps only taken_at. Proposed: the browser sends when the attempt began and the server stores started_at, so time = taken_at minus started_at. Older attempts have none, and with no legacy data that is fine.
  • Which flashcard attempts record: signed-in study of a workspace set, as ratings do today. Embedded sets and signed-out share pages show the summary from the page's own state, with no result page.
  • Score: good = first rating Good or Easy. OK, or should the final rating count?
  • Study these 4: an attempt of only the Hard and Again cards is new. Keep it, or only Study again?
  • Progress page data: one GET /api/workspaces/{id}/study/items with per-item attempt counts, sizes and the last 3 results; the Study tab stays as it is.
