Picked so far: F2 (the whole window painted); no notch, with Search, Notifications and the profile in the sidebar at every width; the page frame chosen in the Theme drawer (profile menu, Theme); cursor flag Z2 (the editor session builds the cursors; C3 is with them and Epo).

New this round: the sidebar on the paint as well, and the Theme drawer as a bottom sheet on phones.

1. The sidebar on the paint

SB1Frosted sidebar

The sidebar keeps its card shape, but the card is 60% white over the paint (55% ink in dark themes), so the paint shows through softly and the text keeps its contrast at any paint strength.

SB2Open sidebar, like Arc

No card: the sidebar's rows sit straight on the window's paint, as in your Arc screenshot. Arc keeps its tint pastel, and so must we: at full strength the dark top of Dusk swallows the section labels (small shot). So with SB2 the presets default to a soft strength, about half white, and the Strength slider (D2) stops before the text loses contrast.

2. Theme drawer: a bottom sheet on phones

On phones the Theme drawer rises from the bottom instead of sliding in from the right. It stops below the top of the page, so you can see the frame change while you pick, and it scrolls when D2's controls run past the screen. Desktop keeps the drawer on the right.

How it is drawn

Arc-style custom colours rule out one pre-rendered image per scene, so every frame is drawn the same way: one 4 KB grey brush texture (the clouds and streaks from round 1, in grey) is laid over a gradient of the chosen colours with an overlay blend, plus a small grain tile. Presets are just colour sets through the same path, so there are no per-scene images. When the choice changes, the browser bakes the result once into an image (a canvas, about 10 ms), and the page then pays only an image's cost. Measured over 20 window resizes at 1440 by 900:

Dark themes darken the result with the same ink wash as before. The saved choice is small: a preset name, or up to three colours plus strength and grain.
