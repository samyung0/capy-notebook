Painted page frame, round 5

Capy Notebook UI mocks, 2026-10-21. Screens are the real app (MSW data) with each change injected into the live page. Desktop is 1440 px, phone is 360 px.

Picked so far: F2 (the whole window painted); no notch, with Search, Notifications and the profile in the sidebar; SB1, the frosted sidebar; D2, presets plus custom colours in the Theme drawer, a bottom sheet on phones; cursor flag Z2 (with the editor session).

New this round: the pattern is its own choice, separate from the colours, with painted as one of six; two designs for Strength and Grain; None brings the sidebar back to normal.

1. Patterns

Pattern and colours are now separate choices: any pattern takes the colour preset or the custom colours. All six use the same cheap drawing (CSS gradients, plus a small grey texture for Painted, Waves and Contours) and are baked into one image when the choice changes. Shown with custom pink, teal and amber.

2. The drawer

Page frame, in order: Pattern (None first), Colours (seven presets, then Custom), the colour field with − and +, then Strength and Grain. Two designs for the last two:

G1Arc's controls

Strength is a wave that grows as it rises and goes flat past the thumb; Grain is a dotted dial that fills as you turn it.

G2Tracks that show their effect

Two sliders whose tracks preview the result: Strength runs from pale to full colour in the chosen colours, Grain from smooth to grainy.

None

With None the window goes back to today's page colour, the sidebar card returns to its normal background, and the drawer hides the colour and strength controls.

3. Try it

Pick a pattern, a preset or Custom, drag the dots or click the field, and drag Strength and Grain (the dial turns). Switch between G1 and G2 above the panel. The preview is the real Dashboard with the frosted sidebar.

Strength is capped so the frosted sidebar keeps its contrast; grain is a static tile, never animated.
