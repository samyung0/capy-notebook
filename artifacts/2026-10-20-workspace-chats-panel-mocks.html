Left panel

Chats: an archive button at the top right, a purple New chat button, then an underline search that filters chat titles (the question bank panel’s field). Files: the same search at the top of the tree. The profile button has no chevron.

Archived chats

Two groups only: Pinned and Older. The small archive button at the top right of the left panel opens the archived chats in a dialog: each row has Delete then Unarchive, and the footer does both for all of them. Delete all asks once in a confirm dialog; a single Delete does not.

Left panel closed

Below 1536px the panel starts closed; the panel button in the viewer header opens it, from 1280px as today. The right panel runs full height and its dropdown holds all three views: Files, Chat, Study. The chat history clock stays here, since there is no list on the left. Drawn at 1366 × 860.

Decided

  1. The left panel opens by default from 1536px; after a toggle the app remembers the choice on that device.
  2. Clicking a chat opens it in the right panel's Chat view (switching from Study if needed); New chat does the same with an empty chat.
  3. Delete all asks once in a confirm dialog; a single Delete does not.
  4. Server: a migration adds pinned_at and archived_at (nullable timestamps) to conversations; the list endpoint returns them, Pin/Archive update them, and the archived dialog gets unarchive-all and delete-all endpoints. Older sorts by updated_at.
  5. The app sidebar on other pages and the phone one-column layout stay as they are.
