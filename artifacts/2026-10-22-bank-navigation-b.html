Question bank navigation: B

Round 4. B with the L1 tree. New this round: strip names follow the workspace card rule, several exams can stay open, and the panel is drawn in both themes. Desktop frames are 1280 wide, scaled to fit; phones are 375 wide. Cover art and the trail map come from the running app.

S · Exam strip

The name colour comes from each cover, as on workspace cards: covers marked light (paper, shelf) get dark text #1d1d1f; the rest get white, with the cards’ bottom shade under busy art (symbols, doodles). Covers look the same in both themes, so today’s dark-mode dimming of paper covers goes. The count and arrow sit on the panel and follow the theme. In dark, the cream paper fades into the panel through a grey band (IELTS); the other option is to keep dimming paper covers in dark, which would then need white text there.

P · Panel in both themes

HKDSE, the exam last practised, starts open. Here the learner also opened IELTS: it stays open, nothing closes on its own.

M · Main page

Continue, Other topics and Finished at the page’s normal gutter. A whole row opens the topic (or its Summary when finished); the second row shows the hover.

  • Other topics: name and exam · subject, the purple bar, “12 of 20”. Finished: name and date.
  • The lead topic keeps “Continue at question 5” under the map.
  • Panel: the last practised exam starts open; ABRSM shows the strip hover.
