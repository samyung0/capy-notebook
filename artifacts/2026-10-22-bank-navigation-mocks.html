Question bank navigation

Four ways to move between exams, subjects, topics and questions on /qb, each without the floating bar and bottom sheet below 1280px. Desktop frames are 1280 wide, scaled to fit; phones are 375 wide at real size. Covers are simplified stand-ins.

  • Today: from xl the right panel swaps between the exam tree and the topic’s question list (back arrow between them). Below xl the same two lists sit in a 70dvh bottom sheet behind a floating bar with Exams / Questions.
  • The two jobs are separable: switching topic (exam → subject → topic) and jumping to a question. Every option answers both; the table at the end shows which pieces mix.

A · Switch from the title recommended

The breadcrumb and the topic title become dropdowns. The right panel only holds the topic’s questions, as a numbered grid instead of 34 near-identical text rows.

  • HKDSE ▾ opens the exam strips (today’s switcher), Mathematics ▾ lists subjects, Area practice ▾ lists the other topics in the subject with progress.
  • Right panel: numbered squares coloured by result (the Summary page’s squares), current question ringed, Filter and Select above, the next question’s first line below.
  • Phone: once the header scrolls away a slim bar sticks with the title ▾ and a 5 / 34 ▾ chip; both open popovers from the top, so nothing sits where you type.
  • Cost: dropdown crumbs are a new pattern; the topic tree with every exam at once disappears from the topic page (it stays on the /qb landing).

B · One outline, everywhere

Keep a side panel, but stop swapping it: one outline holds the exam switcher, subjects, topics and, under the open topic, its question numbers. Phones open the same outline from a button in the top row.

  • No back arrow inside the panel; the question numbers expand under the open topic like a table of contents.
  • Filter and Select sit with the open topic’s numbers.
  • Phone: the top row gets an outline button showing the topic name; it drops the outline as a popover anchored under the row (the workspace Files popover pattern).
  • Cost: a long subject pushes the question grid down; the outline needs its own scroll position kept.

C · Pages, no panel

Exams, subjects and topics become plain pages you walk through: /qb lists exams, an exam page lists every topic by subject, a topic page lists its questions. The right panel goes away and questions get the full width.

  • Exam page: a contents page with progress per topic and Continue / Start.
  • Topic page: a row of numbered squares sticks under the header for jumping; at the end, a link to the next topic.
  • Phone: nothing floats or opens; the back arrow goes up one level.
  • Cost: switching to a topic in another subject is two taps (back, then pick); the number row scrolls sideways on long topics.

D · Jump field

One field does all the moving: type an exam, a topic or a question number. It reuses the ranked exam + topic search the panel already has, plus question numbers. Previous / next topic links close each topic.

  • Desktop: field next to the crumbs, ⌘K focuses it; results grouped as questions, topics, exams.
  • Phone: field in the top row between menu and avatar; results fill the page while the keyboard is up.
  • Cost: browsing without knowing what to type is weaker; works best on top of A or C, not alone.
