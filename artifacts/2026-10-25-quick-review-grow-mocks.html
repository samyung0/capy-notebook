Quick review cards that grow

Quick review becomes a row of compact cards that scrolls sideways, with the same edge fade as other horizontal rows. Clicking a card makes that card itself grow out of the row into a large card centred over the page, like a dialog. Closing shrinks it back into its slot. Try both frames below.

What happens

The row

  • Cards are 200 × 118 (176 wide on phones). The text is cut off after three lines, with the source and type below it.
  • The row scrolls sideways with snapping, and its edges fade only while there is more to scroll. This uses the app's scroll-fade-x.
  • An answered card shows its result for a moment, then its slot closes up. After the last card, the section says it's done.

The enlarged card

  • The card grows from its own position and size to the centre: 520 wide on desktop, the full width minus 16px each side on phones.
  • Its short text fades out while the full card fades in. Flashcards flip with a click, then show Again / Hard / Good / Easy. Questions have Check, then Next.
  • Esc, the ×, or a click on the backdrop shrinks it back into its slot. It behaves as a real dialog: focus stays inside and returns to the card afterwards.

A vs B

  • A · Grow animates the box's size and position. The text never stretches, and the full content is revealed as the box opens. Only this one card's layout updates while it moves.
  • B · Zoom scales the finished card up from the slot, the way a phone app opens. It's cheaper to animate, but the text is stretched for a moment.
  • Opening takes 350ms and closing 250ms, with the app's smooth-out ease. With reduced motion turned on, it only crossfades.
