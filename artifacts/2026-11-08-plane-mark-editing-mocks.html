Moving marks on a plane

Today the open plane only adds and erases marks: a wrong point means erasing and drawing again. Three ways to pick up a point, a line, a curve or a label and move it. Each plane below works: drag, zoom with the wheel or a pinch, and use the ☰ menu's Undo.

AMove picks up marks

HOW IT WORKS
  • The Move tool (as today) grabs whatever is under it: a point's dot moves that point, a line or curve moves all of it, a label moves the label.
  • Pressing empty paper pans, as today.
  • A mark stays picked: blue with its points ringed, and a bin above it. Delete also removes it, Esc lets go.
  • The row stays at six tools.

BA Select tool beside Move

HOW IT WORKS
  • A new Select tool (the arrow, ringed blue here) picks up marks like A. Move becomes a hand that only pans.
  • Dragging over empty paper with Select draws a box that picks every mark inside it, so several move together.
  • Bin, Delete and Esc as in A.
  • Seven tools in the row.

CPoints move with any tool

HOW IT WORKS
  • With any tool, a point's dot can be dragged: it turns blue under the pointer. A tap on it still does the tool's job (Line starts from it).
  • Move also drags whole lines, curves and labels; empty paper pans.
  • Nothing stays picked: no bin, removing stays with Erase.
  • The row stays at six tools.

All three: points land on the grid as they do when drawn, one drag is one undo step, the coordinate chip follows a dragged point, and the board card shows the moved marks when Done closes the plane. My pick is A: nothing new to learn, and Move already means "move things" on the rest of the board.
