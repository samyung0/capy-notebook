Workspace canvas · 2026-11-09 · round 2

Misc panel, round 2

One frame of one size in every view, with loaders where there was nothing. KLIPY gets a Stickers tab. Quiz and Flashcards become a grid of thumbnails with a New tile, and what lands on the canvas is the quiz itself, not a link.

1 · The frame

360 × 440 in every view

The panel no longer sizes itself to its content, so switching from the tiles to a KLIPY search or a quiz list moves nothing. Every view has the same header: Back, the title, and a close. The body scrolls inside the frame.

2 · KLIPY

GIFs and Stickers, one search

KLIPY's sticker API mirrors its GIF one (search and trending, the same filters and sizes, PNG and WebP). The two share the search field and the placeholder KLIPY requires; the tab picks the kind. Stickers show on a checker, since they have no background, and land on the canvas without one. Every state keeps the frame.

3 · Quiz and Flashcards

A grid of thumbnails, New first

The workspace's quizzes and sets as cards, two across, each with its stored preview: the title, the count, the first question's stem and type (a set shows its first card's front). The first tile makes a new one: it creates the quiz in the workspace, places it on the canvas and opens it to edit. A filter field appears above the grid once there are more than six.

4 · On the canvas

The quiz itself, two ways

Today a placed quiz is a link card: title and kind, double-click to open. Both options below show the quiz's content on the canvas; they differ in how much of the quiz runs there.

A · Preview card

Genetics check-in8 questions
  1. Which base pairs with adenine in DNA?
  2. A gene is a stretch of…
  3. Name the enzyme that unzips the double helix.
Open

The card draws the quiz's stored preview: title, count, the first questions' stems (no answers). It resizes, and it reflows as the quiz changes. Open (or a double click) runs the quiz in the workspace as today. Light: one DOM card per quiz, no room joined, draws in the Files preview too.

5 · To decide

Your picks

  1. Placed quiz: A (preview card, Open runs it in the workspace) or B (the live quiz on the canvas, A for the ones out of view). Flashcards follow the same choice: A shows the first card's front, B flips the deck in place.
  2. New quiz from the panel: create it in the workspace (unfiled), place the card, and open the editor right away. Same for a new set.
  3. Frame: 360 × 440 for every view, Back and close in the header; on a 360 px phone it spans the width.
  4. Stickers: a tab beside GIFs in the same KLIPY view, under the one Misc tile (GIF becomes "GIF & Sticker"), or a Sticker tile of its own opening the same view on its tab, as drawn.
