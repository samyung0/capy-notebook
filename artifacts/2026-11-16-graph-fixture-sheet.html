Graph fixture sheet: JSXGraph and the engine

Every shared graph fixture drawn twice: on the left by JSXGraph (the stored-SVG renderer until 16 Nov 2026), on the right by the engine's graphSvg (src/features/graph/), in the canvas plane's look (D5, D14). Both were captured in headless Chromium on 16 Nov 2026 from the fixtures' recipes; the stored SVGs in the fixtures are not used. Exports are on the light paper whatever the app theme.

What changes in the engine's pictures:

  • The plane's grid: minor lines between the numbered ones, lighter grey; the axes in the plane's grey at 1.5 px with numbers haloed in the paper.
  • Axis numbers leave with their axis (D14): a view that leaves out an axis shows none of its numbers (JSXGraph pinned the axis to the nearer edge).
  • Ink is black at 2 px with round ends; points are 4.5 px dots; dashes 7/5 px; shading 15% ink.
  • Labels and angle values are 15 px Arial (JSXGraph: 12 px); a label is centred on its point, as a plane label is (JSXGraph: its left edge, middle), and wraps at 200 px.
  • Angle arcs and the right-angle square keep a fixed 24 px radius at any zoom (JSXGraph: 6% of the view).
  • A frame line round the graph, as on a plane card.
  • The engine also draws recipes JSXGraph's checks refused (it draws what it can; the validators still refuse them).
