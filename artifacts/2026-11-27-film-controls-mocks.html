Film controls

Every film gets Capy's own controls, drawn over the film, so a drawn film (ManimGX on a canvas) and a video film (<video>) look and work the same. The bar is split into the film's chapters: the scene's sections when it has several, otherwise one per animation, named by the line of code that played it, as ManimGX does now.

A through C keep the controls over the film; D, after your reference, puts them under it with the slides beside it. A film with steps stops at the end of each step and waits, like a slide. A film without steps plays straight through. Every player below works: press play, step through, click the bar. The note films are 28 s long.

How it works
  • Keys: Space or K play/pause, → / ← next and previous step (or 5 s without steps), F full screen.
  • Between steps the bar has a wider gap; chapters inside a step keep the thin one.
  • The label reads “Step 2 of 4 · Cut it into rings”; without steps, the chapter's name (“Line 19”).
  • Closest to what viewers already know from YouTube; nothing outside the film.

Option D

Course player: controls under the film, slides beside it

The layout from your reference (it matches iSpring's course player), without the presenter's camera. Nothing covers the film. A thin line over the bar shows how far into the slide it is; the slides list sits beside the film where there's room, and behind the slide count where there isn't. Click the film to play or pause. The bar's buttons have no border, only a hover; ⋮ holds Captions and the drawn-only Theme.

How it works
  • Slides are the scene's sections. The film stops at the end of each; Next (or →) plays the next, ‹ goes back to the start of this one or the one before.
  • Drag the knob (or press anywhere on the line) to seek; a playing film carries on from where it's let go. The line covers the current slide; on a film without slides it covers the whole film, with a mark per chapter, and the time shows beside the buttons.
  • 1× opens 0.25× to 2×, ManimGX's own speeds. ⋮ › Captions shows the scene's captions (Manim's add_subcaption), here one per slide.
  • ⋮ › Theme: Film (the scene's own colours) or Paper (the note's light page). Drawn films only; a video film has no Theme.
  • The list collapses with » on wide screens; the slide count opens it as a menu otherwise.

Open questions

  1. Where steps come from. Recommended: the scene's sections (Manim CE's self.next_section("Cut it into rings"), which ManimGX already records), with the exporter's --steps flag making the film stop at each one. The other way is a Capy-only call like Manim Slides' next_slide(), which scenes written for plain Manim would lack.
  2. Can a viewer play a stepped film straight through? Manim Slides and Motion Canvas can't; YouTube never stops. A could add “Play through” to a ⋮ menu if wanted.
  3. Sound and speed. ManimGX films can carry sound; a volume button would appear only on films that have it. No speed control in any option yet.

What it takes to build, any option: ManimGX's fork adds a way to hide its own controls and hands Capy the chapters (a few lines in web.rs); the exporter writes the chapters and steps into the film's header so video films keep them (today only drawn films do); Capy draws the controls over both players.
