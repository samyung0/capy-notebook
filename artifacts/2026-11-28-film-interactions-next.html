Not in the lesson

  • Phone gestures (page): one finger drags or orbits, two fingers turn and pinch to scale or zoom.
  • Keyboard (page): Tab to a grabbable object, arrows move it, Shift-arrows turn it; in 3D, arrows orbit.
  • Results into study progress (page and server): a checked task counts like a quiz question in Capy's study tracking.
  • Live recomputation (needs the scene's Python): a label that recomputes for any position, not just precomputed stops. That's the same problem as sliders for scene parameters, set aside for later.
