What actually changes

Geometry stays the same. The repository generates a separate SVG for each flavor, replacing the stroke and fill colors with that flavor's palette.

Likely fit for Capy

Latte is the light-mode candidate. Macchiato or Mocha fits a dark interface. Frappé is softer and less contrasty than both.
