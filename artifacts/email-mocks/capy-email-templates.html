Clerk sends

Clerk keeps sending these, so they never use our quota. Clerk lets anyone edit templates on a development instance, but editing them on staging or production instances needs a paid Clerk plan. Clerk's editor uses its own block markup (Revolvapp), so these mocks get rebuilt in that editor. Each template has one language. The first two are confirmed from our sign-up and reset code. The last two are Clerk notices that may or may not be turned on; check the dashboard.

Stripe sends

Receipts and payment problems

Stripe can send receipts, failed-payment notices and card-expiry reminders for us. They don't use our quota. Turn them on under Settings → Customer emails, then set the look under Settings → Branding:

  • Icon and logo: the Capy mark (the same PNG the emails will use).
  • Brand colour #222222 (buttons), accent #8c7bd9.
  • The Pro started email above says Stripe sends the receipt, so that copy depends on receipts being on.

Decisions before building

  1. Office maintenance (decided). The email went to every account once per upgrade; the --reminder run was already in-app only. It now goes only to owners and editors of a workspace with an Office file edited in the last 30 days, and everyone still gets the in-app notices.
  2. Clerk paid plan. Restyling Clerk mail on UAT and production needs a paid Clerk plan. Without one, only the development instance gets the new look.
  3. When welcome sends. Suggest after onboarding finishes rather than on Clerk's user.created webhook, so we have the person's name and language. One email per sign-up.
  4. Pro cancelled shows usage only when it matters. The "You use 640 MB now" line should only show for accounts over 100 MB. The Maily build rejects conditional blocks today, so this needs either two template variants or Go {{if}} support in the build.
  5. Logo hosting. Gmail doesn't render SVG, so the mark needs a PNG at a stable public URL (for example on the app host) before these ship.
  6. Pro ending. No separate "Pro has ended" email: accounts over the Free limit already get the over-quota email, and everyone else was told the date when they cancelled.
