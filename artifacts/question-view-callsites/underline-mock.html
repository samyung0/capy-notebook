Notes on the mock

  • Row heights stay at 44px, so hit targets don't shrink when the box goes away.
  • Checked colours stay on the rule and the key badge only. The tinted fills are gone; "missed" is a dashed green rule, so it reads differently from "your answer, right".
  • Skipping records nothing for review: no rating, no history entry. A quiz question left wholly blank scores 0 marks but is not rated either; a question with an ordering part is never blank.
  • Read-only previews (question editor, dialog) would use the answering look with nothing selected.
  • Matching on phones has no dropdown or sheet: the options are listed once above the items, and the page scrolls if they are long.
