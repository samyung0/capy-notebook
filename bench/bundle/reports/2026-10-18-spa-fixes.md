# SPA bundle budget: first limits (2026-10-18)

Brotli-11 KB (1024 bytes) per `bench/bundle/scripts/budget.mjs`, on this
Windows PC with the shared install (`land-cutover-lock`: mathlive 0.110.0,
slate-react 0.126.0, against the lockfile's 0.111.1 and 0.126.4) and the
BetterOffice pin 79138d8d.

- **main 33aff569b** and **fixes** are production client builds with the
  deploy settings a release has (Clerk key, Sentry DSN, PostHog key, Office
  runtime origin, bucket): main with the manifest and chunk list added by a
  local config, fixes the `editor/spa-fixes` branch.
- **fixes, CI build** is the branch as `frontend_check` builds it, with no
  deploy settings: no Sentry DSN, so Sentry's setup and the lazy Replay chunk
  drop out of the bundle, and the entry is about 26 KB smaller than a
  deployed build's. The limits are these numbers plus a little headroom
  (about 4 %), since that is the build CI measures.

Never-in rules: main breaks ten (Yjs and Replay in the entry; MSAL on the
dashboard, workspace list, Explore and workspace pages; the quiz runner, card
view and image zoom on the file page; Plate in the DOCX, PPTX and XLSX
views); both fix builds break none. A build with Yjs imported by the entry
fails the step on the entry's bytes and on the Yjs rule.

The routes beyond the entry grow where the entry shrank: Zod (about 72 KB
minified) and the generated validators a page uses moved from the entry into
the shared chunks of the pages that build forms (Billing, Files, Settings,
QuestionBank and others). The file page (MaterialOpen) halves: the material
views, the empty workspace's cards, the image viewer and the text source view
load with their kinds.
