# Make a quiz: first cut on HKDSE past papers (2026-11-03)

The first bench of question extraction from uploaded papers
(`todo-study-tracking.md` §8, step 7): the production pipeline
(`pipeline/retrieval/extraction.py`) over three HKDSE Mathematics papers,
scored against hand-built gold, with DeepSeek Flash (Epo's preference) and
GLM-5.3-Flash. Mac, 2026-11-03, 17:20–18:30 JST.

## Setup

- **What runs is production code.** `extraction.run`: the outline from the
  pages' indexed text, windows of two page images with `record_question` /
  `continue_question`, every recorded question through Go's validator, the
  answer file parsed as text beside the windows, pairing by label in code.
  The model calls go through `models.complete` with no accounting session (as
  offline tests do) and the service's call-slot policy bound.
- **Stand-ins for what the app would have.** Pages render with the capture
  renderer (1,568 px JPEG). A page's indexed text is the PDF text layer, or,
  for a scan (all three papers and both marking schemes are scans), the
  production VLM page read on the captioning pin (`deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017`
  through OpenRouter, grey 1,280 px), cached and not counted below. The
  validator is `server/cmd/quizcheck` (what quiz creation runs).
- **Models.** `deepseek/deepseek-flash` on DeepSeek's own API, thinking `low`
  for windows and off for the outline and scheme; `zai/glm-5.3-flash` through
  OpenRouter at `low` (it cannot turn reasoning off). Credits use the catalog
  rates (DeepSeek 600 / 12 / 2,400 micros per input / cached / output token,
  GLM 300 / 60 / 1,000); a credit is $0.0005.
- **DeepSeek takes images.** Before building, a probe sent a page image with
  thinking `low` and a tool to DeepSeek's API: it read the page and called the
  tool (about 1,050 prompt tokens for a 1,280 px page). GLM did the same.

## Papers and gold

Gold is in `fixtures/gold/` (labels, part labels, per-part marks, answer type;
for multiple choice the key and a signature of the keyed option).

| Paper | Pages sent | Answers sent | Gold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2022 Paper 2 | all 16 (cover and blank last page included) | a one-line-per-item answer list built from the gold keys (`1. D`) | 45 MCQ, 1 mark each; keys from `2022-p2-key.json` (HKEAA report via mathseasy.hk); keyed options' signatures hand-read from the pages; 10 items need a figure |
| 2021 Paper 1 | 2–8 (Section A(1) and question 10) | `2021-ans.pdf`, 14 pages (scheme and examiners' report) | 10 questions, 19 parts, all open; part labels and marks from the scheme (per-year catalogue, spot-checked on the pages); 2 need a figure |
| 2024 Paper 1 | 2–8 (same) | `2024-ans.pdf`, 10 pages | 10 questions, 17 parts; 1 figure |

Paper 1 is cut to pages 2–8 because a quiz holds at most 20 open parts
(`QUIZ_OPEN_PARTS_MAX`); a whole Paper 1 has 42–47 (finding 5).

The option-order check: for each multiple-choice item the score takes the
option at the stored key's index and compares its signature (numbers, signs,
relations, roman numerals, trig names) with the hand-read signature of the
printed correct option. A reordered option list would point the key at
another option.

- Label precision was 1.00 everywhere: no invented questions, the P2 cover
  and blank page recorded nothing, and the outline's expected labels matched
  the gold every time.
- Reasoning at `low` reasons little: 640–980 reasoning tokens a DeepSeek run.
- Repeats of a paper hit DeepSeek's prompt cache across runs, so their cost is
  low. The first run of each paper is the honest cost: **2022 P2 50.3
  credits ($0.025, 79k of 111k input tokens cached within the run), 2021 P1
  32.4, 2024 P1 33.9**. With no cache at all the same token counts would cost
  about 90 (P2) and 55 (P1) credits.
- The 165–278 s runs were DeepSeek's API, not the pipeline (finding 4).

## Findings

1. **DeepSeek Flash reads the papers well.** Every label on all three papers
   in every run, every MCQ's options in printed order with the key on the
   printed correct option (90/90), part structure 99/100 on Paper 1, every
   saved figure-bearing question boxed. Paper 2 (45 MCQ, 16 pages) saved all 45
   with the right keys in both runs.
2. **The weak link is the mark scheme parse, not the pages.** Every Paper 1
   question that was not saved, and every wrong part mark, comes from the
   scheme entries:
   - 2024 Q3(b): the parse labelled it `b(ii)` in 4 of 5 runs (the page says
     (b)); exact label pairing then finds no entry and the question is left
     out (`unanswered`). A probe of the same text labelled it `b` in 3 of 3
     tries, so it is noise, not the text.
   - 2021 Q4: the VLM read put the marks of (a) and (b) in one table cell
     (1M 1A 1A 1A); the parse gave all four to (a). (b) then took its own 1 in
     4 of 5 runs, so the question carries 5 marks where the paper prints 4,
     and in 1 run (b) had none and was left out. The run now reports such
     questions (`report.marksDiffer`, added after these runs).
   - One 2024 run (run 1, before entries were kept in the results) left out
     5 questions at once.
   - Case marking (2021 Q8: Case 1 two marks, Case 2 one) was summed to 3
     until the prompt said to take the highest case; alternative methods are
     taken first-only.
3. **GLM is cheaper per run but worse,** and 1.5–2× slower: it misread 21 of
   90 keys from a plain answer list, matched the Paper 1 part structure in
   only 27 of 40 question-runs (short answers where the paper wants working,
   merged parts), and once summed alternative methods (2024 Q2 and Q6 at
   double marks). DeepSeek stays the default.
4. **DeepSeek's API was overloaded during the run.** From about 18:05 JST it
   answered "Service is too busy" and let streams go silent for 120 s. Before
   the fix seven runs died that way, each after spending credits; with three
   attempts per window, scheme piece and outline (20 s, then 40 s apart, now
   in the pipeline) four runs went through in 165–278 s. A background job on
   one provider needs that, and may need a second route (GLM, or 1017 through
   OpenRouter) when DeepSeek is down.
5. **Quiz caps cut papers short.** A quiz holds 20 open parts and 100 parts;
   a whole HKDSE Paper 1 has 42–47 open parts, so a run stops when the quiz
   is full (`stopped: quiz_full`) after about half the paper. Running the rest
   "into the same quiz" (plan §8) is impossible under the cap.
6. **Cost and time for 40 pages.** First runs cost 3.1 credits a page on Paper
   2 with its answer list, and 4.6 a page on Paper 1 with its 10–14 page
   scheme. A 40-page run would be about 125–185 credits (more where the
   provider's cache misses) and, at Paper 2's pace of 4.4 s a page, about 3
   minutes when the provider is healthy. Paper 1's 7 pages with a 14-page
   scheme cost 32 credits against the plan's estimate of 90–180 for a 12-page
   paper with an 8-page scheme; one 40-page paper still takes most of a Free
   month's 200 credits.
7. **Figures are found but not kept.** The model boxed every figure the gold
   lists (10/10 on P2) but crops are not made this round, so geometry items
   are saved without their diagram.

## Left out

- Figure crops (boxes are recorded only), blind-solving keys and worked
  solutions a paper lacks (plan step 5), Office pages and Markdown sections,
  the quiz being hidden from members (no material-level flag exists).
- Gold covers labels, parts, marks, types and MCQ keys, not the transcribed
  text or the marking items' wording.
- No run measured the production gateway, retrieval service and database end
  to end (Docker was not running): the bench calls the pipeline in-process.

## Reproduce

```sh
python bench/extraction/scripts/run.py --paper 2022-p2 --model deepseek --repeat 1 \
  --refs data/question-bank/references/hkdse-math-compulsory --env-file <keys file>
```

`--score-only` rescores a saved result. Spend for every run above, the
shakedown runs, the failed runs, the probes and 54 VLM page reads: under
$0.50.
