Two AI-built study materials answer the same learner request. You see the request and both materials' markdown, labelled First and Second. You do not know how either was built.

Judge which serves the learner better on three things, each as first, second or tie:
- coherence: the explanation leads into worked examples and practice that come from the same material and use one consistent notation; definitions, examples and exercises refer to each other instead of standing apart; nothing is repeated or contradicted across sections.
- completeness: everything the request asked for is there at the depth the request implies, and nothing important for understanding the topic is skipped.
- level fit: the language, prerequisites and difficulty match the learner described in the request.
Then give an overall verdict and two or three sentences of reasons that name concrete places in the materials.

Do not judge factual correctness; a separate check does that. Do not reward length for its own sake. Say tie when the difference would not matter to a learner.
